Privacy Policy

Effective July 22, 2026

BetterMe is built to run almost entirely on your device. This page explains, plainly, what the app can access, what actually leaves your phone, and what never does.

01 Overview

BetterMe has no account system — no email, no password, no sign-in. There is nothing to hack because there's no central database of users to begin with. Your goals, habits, journal entries, meals, and style catalog live in local storage on your device. A small number of features send a request to our own backend server to generate an AI response — those are called out explicitly below, and nothing else leaves your phone.

02 Profile & onboarding

During setup you can optionally enter your name, age, school/grade, sex, height, weight, activity level, business or goals you're working toward, and a daily schedule. This is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults and SwiftData) and is used only to personalize the app — calculating calorie targets, generating an AI schedule, tailoring the AI coach's tone. None of it is transmitted to us as an identity or account record.

03 Health data

Read-only

If you grant HealthKit access, BetterMe reads your step count, active energy, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability to power the Kinetic dashboard. The app requests read-only access — it does not write workouts, body metrics, or anything else back to Apple Health. Health data is used entirely on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or anyone else's.

04 Camera & photos

Wardrobe can use your camera to scan clothing items and your photo library to add existing photos. Images are analyzed on-device (Apple's Vision framework identifies dominant colors and item type) to catalog the piece. Photos you save stay in the app's local storage on your device.

05 Your content & AI features

Journal entries, logged meals and workouts, habits, and closet items are stored locally. A few features are AI-powered and work by sending a request to our backend (hosted on Railway) to generate a response:

AI Coach chat

Your message and recent conversation history are sent to generate a reply.

AI schedule builder

Your stated goals, wake/sleep times, and tasks are sent to generate a daily plan.

AI course builder

The topic you want to learn is sent to generate lessons and exercises.

AI outfit & workout generation

A description of your items or preferences is sent to generate a suggestion.

These requests are used solely to generate the response shown to you. We do not use this content for advertising, and we do not sell it. We do not tie these requests to any account, since none exists.

06 Notifications

Reminders (like a streak-at-risk nudge) are scheduled locally on your device using iOS's notification system. BetterMe does not operate a push-notification server and cannot send you a message remotely.

07 Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers, and BetterMe does not embed third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The only outside party involved is our own backend (for the AI features above) and, if you use Wardrobe's redeem-code feature, a separate Night.inc service that validates purchase codes from 2AM.

08 Retention & deletion

Because almost everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. There's no server-side account to separately close. If you'd like us to confirm deletion of anything transmitted through an AI request (which we do not retain beyond generating the response), you can reach out using the contact info below.

09 Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Local data is protected by the same encryption and sandboxing every iOS app relies on. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we don't ask you to trust a central database with your information in the first place.

10 Children's privacy

BetterMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information through the app's AI features, contact us and we'll address it.

11 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data the app accesses or how it's used, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the app.
